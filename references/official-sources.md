# Official sources bookmark (India + abroad)

Use these for **verification**. URLs can change; search the body name if a link breaks.

## NEET (UG)

- **NTA NEET** — notifications, result, information bulletin for the **exam year**.

## All India / central counselling

- **Medical Counselling Committee (MCC)** — [mcc.nic.in](https://mcc.nic.in)  
  AIQ, central institutes, deemed universities as per **current** NEET UG brochure.

## Gujarat (state)

- **ACPUGMEC / medical admission Gujarat** — [medadmgujarat.org](http://www.medadmgujarat.org/)  
  UG medical/dental state counselling; read the **Information Booklet** for the academic year.  
  Published **UG contact / helpline** page (verify each cycle): [medadmgujarat.org/ug/contact.aspx](https://www.medadmgujarat.org/ug/contact.aspx)

## Medical regulation (India)

- **National Medical Commission (NMC)** — [nmc.org.in](https://www.nmc.org.in)  
  - Studying abroad: **Information desk → For students to study in abroad** (criteria, recognized institutions, eligibility).  
  - Indian MBBS / registration rules as applicable.

**Supplementary (global catalogue, not Indian approval):** [World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS)](https://www.wdoms.org) — cross-check school metadata only.

## Screening / licentiate (foreign MBBS graduates)

- Follow **NMC** and **NBE** (National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences) **official notices** for the relevant year (e.g. FMGE / transition to **NEXT** — naming and rules evolve; do not rely on this file’s static wording alone).

## Maharashtra (state — NEET UG)

- **State CET Cell** — [mahacet.org](https://www.mahacet.org/) (NEET UG medical/dental CAP portals update **per academic year**).
- **Directorate of Medical Education & Research (DMER)** — [dmer.maharashtra.gov.in](https://dmer.maharashtra.gov.in/) (admission notices, links to brochure).

## Karnataka (state — NEET UG)

- **Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)** — [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/) (open the **current** UG NEET path for your admission year).

## Other states

- For any state not listed above: that state’s **official** medical counselling host and **PDF bulletin only**.
