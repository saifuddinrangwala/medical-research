## Contact columns — how to read them

- **Phone / Admissions email:** Populated only where a **quick public reference** was used in this pass; **—** means *confirm on the college’s official site* or **[NMC search](https://www.nmc.org.in/information-desk/college-and-course-search/)** for your admission year.
- **Google Maps:** Opens a predefined **Maps search** (`api=1&query=…`). Correct the pin if a campus shares a generic name.
- **Maharashtra counselling (CET Cell):** Reverified yearly — start at **[cetcell.mahacet.org/contact/](https://cetcell.mahacet.org/contact/)** and **[medicalug…mahacet.org](https://www.mahacet.org/)** UG notices. Numbers such as technical **8447692612** and Mumbai boardlines **022-22016157 / 22016159** appear in public notices (**verify live**).
- **Karnataka counselling (KEA):** **[kea.kar.nic.in](https://kea.kar.nic.in)** / **`cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea`** — commonly cited helpline **080-23460460** (often 10:00–18:00, **verify**).

## 4. Practical notes for Gujarat General candidates

1. Maharashtra / Karnataka **state government seats** mostly need **local domicile** rules — check each brochure.
2. **Private / deemed** pathways may admit **All India** students at **high fees**; confirm on **seat matrix**.
3. **AFMC Pune** ≠ normal MCC state choice filling.
4. **NIMHANS** etc.: confirm **UG MBBS intake pattern** yearly.
5. **Women-only** MBBS colleges are **not listed** here (male applicant).
