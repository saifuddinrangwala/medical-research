**Current instruction preference:** **English medium only** — shortlist and compare only **programmes that the faculty documents as English-taught** (for the **whole** course: pre-clinical + clinical + examinations as applicable). Egyptian public faculties often run **parallel Arabic-national and English/international programmes**; capacity, fees, and eligibility differ. **Scope:** “Cairo” in common agent usage often includes **6th of October City** (Giza Governorate), where several private medical schools sit on the edge of the capital region. This note covers **institutions primarily searched as Cairo/Egypt metro** that award (or historically award) **undergraduate medicine** leading to **`M.B., B.Ch.` / MBBS-equivalent**. Always confirm the **exact programme**, **language of instruction**, and **clinical years** from the faculty’s official pages.

## 1. Are Egyptian medical degrees “valid in India”?

**There is no simple yes/no per university name** in official Indian Materials that says “Cairo University = automatically valid.” Practice in India depends on:

1. **National Medical Commission (NMC)** rules for **foreign medical graduates** (FMGs), **notably** the *Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021* and any **later amendments / notices** applying to **your year of admission** and **completion**. Read the **[NMC page for students wishing to study abroad](https://www.nmc.org.in/information-desk/for-students-to-study-in-abroad/)** and any linked PDFs — they are updated; this file cannot replace them.
2. **NEET eligibility** tied to admission abroad (Indian citizens: follow current NMC/NTA wording for EC / NEET-validity periods).
3. **Course characteristics** spelled out in NMC FMGL schedules (historically discussed in public summaries: minimum **clinical / total course duration**, **English-medium** requirement where applicable, **internship/clinical clerkship alignment**, etc.). The **fine print changes** — your batch must match the **commission’s notification**, not blogs.
4. After qualification: clearance of whatever **national licentiate / screening pathway** operates at that time (historically **FMGE**; **NEXT** trajectory has been communicated in phases — verify with **NBEMS / NMC** notices at qualification time).
5. **Recognition in Egypt**: the awarding university must be a **proper degree-granting medical faculty** authorised under **Egyptian higher‑education / health regulation** so you can Register there as required under NMC FMGL wording.

**Bottom line:** A degree from **Cairo University, Ain Shams, etc.** can still **fail** the Indian pathway if **your specific batch / programme structure** does not match FMGL criteria, paperwork is incomplete, or screening isn’t cleared. Conversely, **Indian authorities do not “accredit Egyptian universities” by name list** in the old MCI style for new admissions — assessment is **regulation + documentation + examinations**.

Auxiliary sanity check only (not Indian legal approval): WHO’s **[World Directory of Medical Schools](https://www.wdoms.org)** entries help confirm global programme metadata — still **does not substitute** NMC compliance.

## 5. Due-diligence checklist (India-focused, before accepting an Egyptian MBBS seat)

1. Obtain / retain **official programme brochure PDF** naming **degree title**, total **years**, **language**, **clinical rotation sites**, and **internship** structure aligned with **[NMC FMGL](https://www.nmc.org.in/information-desk/for-students-to-study-in-abroad/)** wording that applies **to your intake year**.
2. Secure **Eligibility Certificate / NEET-compliance** paperwork per NMC (don’t rely on agents).
3. Match your institution’s **medicine programmes** inside **[WDOMS](https://www.wdoms.org)** (`SchoolDetail` PDF / metadata) — still not an Indian endorsement, only a catalogue cross-check.
4. Plan financially for **screening/licentiates**, possible **additional internship** rules in India, and **visa / residence** realities in Egypt.
5. Prefer **calling the deanery / registrar email on the *.edu.eg*** domain—not random WhatsApp “admission desks.”
