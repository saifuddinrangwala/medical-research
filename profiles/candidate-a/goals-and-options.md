# Goals and option scope

## Primary aspiration

- **First preference:** **MBBS** (India and, if viable, abroad such as **Egypt** or other countries **after NMC verification**).

## Explicitly keep open (do not rule out)

- **BDS**
- **AYUSH** courses (e.g. BAMS, BHMS, BUMS — exact choices to refine when counselling brochures are out)

Rationale: preserve **parallel viable pathways** until **real NEET rank** and **official cutoffs** for 2026 are known.

## Geographic / institutional scope

### India

- **Gujarat** state counselling (**General** merit).
- **Other states / national pools** where eligible — especially **AIQ** and institutional routes via **MCC**, plus **state-private/open quotas** only as **official bulletins** allow for non-domicile candidates.

### Outside India

- **Egypt** called out as an example destination.
- **Language of instruction — current choice:** **English medium only** for overseas MBBS options (for now). Arabic-medium or bilingual tracks are **out of scope** unless the family revisits this later.
- **Other countries** remain under consideration **only if**:

  - the **specific university / intake year** meets **National Medical Commission (NMC)** requirements for studying medicine abroad **as notified for that year**;
  - the offered **programme is credibly English-medium** end-to-end (theory + exams + clinical exposure as stated in **official faculty documents** — required to align with typical **NMC FMGL** expectations for many newer batches; confirm against the **live FMGL PDF**);
  - **NEET eligibility** and any **Eligibility Certificate** rules applicable to admission year are satisfied;
  - return-to-India pathway (e.g. screening / licensure norms as notified at time of qualification) is understood **from official notices**, not agent brochures alone.

## Financial and non-academic constraints

- **Language for abroad:** **English medium** (see above).
- **Not documented** yet: budget ceiling, willingness for **private / institutional (rupee)** fees in India, willingness to relocate. **Add in a future edit** — they strongly change shortlists. (Budget matrices now include **NRI/overseas-tariff ₹** where published; **seat eligibility** for those pools remains **brochure-specific**.)

## Strategic priorities for future assistants

1. After NEET scorecard: treat **AIR** + **category (General)** as primary drivers for **MCC + ACPUGMEC** realistic bands.
2. Maintain **MBBS-first** counselling order while retaining **honest backups** (BDS/AYUSH) until outcomes are settled.
3. For **abroad**, never recommend a university without tying to **NMC official abroad-student guidance** and **named institution + batch year**; for this candidate, prioritise **English-medium** tracks with **written** faculty confirmation (not agent PDFs alone).
