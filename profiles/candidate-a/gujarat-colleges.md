- **Phone / Admissions email:** Filled only when a **specific public source** was used in this pass (college site, university admissions, or widely cited government contact). ## 4. What to do next (for counselling)

2. Cross-check every MBBS name on **NMC** for **that academic year**.
3. **Re-copy Phone / Email** from each institute’s **live** contact page before calling or mailing.
